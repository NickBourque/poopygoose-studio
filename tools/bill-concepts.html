Traced from the creator's drawing (source/bill/bill-sketch.jpg, his master) into vector art (source/bill/bill-34.svg) by tools/trace-bill.html, as the goose was traced from the logo. Beside the goose at game scale, with the same sticker border.

Earlier: three takes

Beside the goose at game scale (Bill about 80% of his height). Same ink, outline weight and sticker border as the goose.

  1. A. Mallard. Teal-green head, white collar, chestnut chest, warm grey-brown body, a blue flash on the wing, a yellow bill. Reads as "duck" at a glance; adds new colours to the palette.
  2. B. Butter. Pale butter-yellow all over, the goose's orange bill and feet. The classic duck: warm, simple, and the closest to the goose's palette.
  3. C. Grey suit. Blue-grey all over, muted orange bill, round spectacles. A duck who has seen it all; the most deadpan, and the furthest from the goose.
